# SmartCSE Assist — Technical Writing Classwork

## About This Project

SmartCSE Assist is a proposed AI-powered student support application developed as part of my first Technical Writing classwork. The concept focuses on using Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help students with academic planning, course recommendations, assignment reminders, basic programming support, and frequently asked academic questions.

## Project Objectives

- To propose an AI-based solution for student academic support.
- To explore how AI can assist students with academic planning and personalized recommendations.
- To practice technical proposal writing, research, and documentation.
- To develop my skills in organizing technical information and presenting ideas in a professional format.

## Contents

- **Technical Proposal:** A document describing the proposed application, its features, workflow, expected benefits, and limitations.
- **System Workflow:** A visual representation of the proposed application's basic workflow.
- **Feature Comparison Table:** A comparison of AI-based features, required inputs, AI techniques, and expected benefits.
- **References:** Sources used to support the proposed system and its features.

## Learning Purpose

This project represents my initial attempt at technical writing. Through this assignment, I aim to improve my academic writing, research, citation, formatting, and technical communication skills.

## Disclaimer

As this is my first Technical Writing classwork, the document may contain errors, inaccuracies, incomplete explanations, or formatting and citation issues. The proposed application is a conceptual idea for academic purposes and has not been presented as a fully developed or tested system. The content may be revised and improved as I gain more knowledge and experience in technical writing and research.
